# Scroll Motion Reference Notes

## Reference observations

The supplied landing page establishes motion through a deep hero-to-product transition: a large workstation visual occupies the upper scroll area, then yields to a spacious section introduction and alternating feature stories. Product frames feel layered against the background rather than moving as a single flat column.

## Trade Fusion translation

Trade Fusion will use original motion rather than copying the reference implementation. The landing page should retain its TF brand system and add three complementary effects: a gentle scroll-linked lift and depth adjustment for the main workspace preview; directional, staggered reveal of alternating product spotlights; and subtle background-grid drift. These effects must use transform and opacity only, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and avoid hiding essential content.
